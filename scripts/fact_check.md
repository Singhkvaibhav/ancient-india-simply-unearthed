# Fact check: what was compared, what changed

Method: contested or number-heavy claims were searched on the web and compared with what the narration said. The session could search but could not open most pages (Wikipedia, World History Encyclopedia and similar hosts were blocked for page fetches), so findings come from search-result summaries, not from reading each source in full. Most results are encyclopaedic or study-guide pages, not primary editions. Claims not listed here are standard textbook facts that were **not** re-verified.

## Checked and left as is

- Mature Harappan phase c. 2600 to 1900 BCE (Ep 4 to 6): standard figure, boundaries vary slightly ([INFLIBNET chapter](https://ebooks.inflibnet.ac.in/antp03/chapter/indus-valley-civilization/)). The Ep 4 note that some books give c. 2500 to 1750 BCE was not confirmed by this search; treat it as unverified.
- Mahavira: Jain tradition 599 to 527 BCE; many modern scholars about 540 to 468 BCE (Ep 11 says both) ([Wikipedia: Mahavira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahavira), [RE Online](https://www.reonline.org.uk/?p=5084)).
- Ashoka's Thirteenth Rock Edict: 150,000 deported, 100,000 killed. These are the edict's own figures and are likely inflated ([Wikipedia: Kalinga War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalinga_War), [McKay sources](https://digfir-published.macmillanusa.com/mckaysources/mckaysources_ch03_8.html)).
- William Jones linked Sandrocottus and Chandragupta in his Tenth Anniversary Discourse, February 1793 ([Times Higher Education](https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/90061)).

## Not verified in this pass

Everything else, including the dynasty dates for the Satavahanas, Pallavas, Cholas and Rajputs, the Gupta and Harsha dates, the Fa-Hien and Hiuen Tsang travel dates, and the quiz answers. These follow standard NCERT-level accounts from memory. Please spot-check them against NCERT and a university text such as Romila Thapar's *Early India* before publishing.
