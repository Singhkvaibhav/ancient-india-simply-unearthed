SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers another copper plate, with a seal still attached. We are in the seventh century now, and for the king this episode is about we have something rare in ancient Indian history: two long written accounts, by two very different people, of the same reign. What do you do when both your sources want to flatter?

Episode 31 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Two writers, one king — Bana at court, Hiuen Tsang travelling. Read them against each other.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
