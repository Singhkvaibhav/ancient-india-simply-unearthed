TITLE
The Tripartite Struggle | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 34

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a temple that is below the ground around it. Builders start at the bottom and go up. Whoever made this started at the top of a hillside and cut downward, removing the rock around the temple until the building was left standing in a courtyard of its own. Nothing was added. Everything was taken away. How do you build a temple by taking a hillside away?
