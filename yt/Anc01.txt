TITLE
How We Know About Ancient India | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 1

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A brush uncovers a scrap of silver, stamped with marks. Beside it, a page of palm leaf. One was money. One was writing. Neither says who made it. So here is the question this whole series rests on: how does anyone know what happened three thousand years ago?

Episode 1 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Five sources, five words. Dig, read, carve, coin, visit.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
