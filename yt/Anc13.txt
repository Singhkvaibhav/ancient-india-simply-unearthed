The Rise of Magadha | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 13

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush clears the cut face of a wall — earth, brick, and the stumps of a timber palisade. Walls like this are expensive. Somebody had a great deal of money, and a reason to be afraid. This episode is about how one state out of sixteen ended up with both. Who had this much money, and what were they afraid of?

Episode 13 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Four reasons Magadha won. Iron, rivers, elephants — and two capitals, each chosen for its moment.
