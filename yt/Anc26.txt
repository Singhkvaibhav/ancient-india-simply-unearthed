TITLE
Sangam Literature and Society | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 26

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers palm leaves covered in small, careful Tamil script. Palm leaf does not last in a tropical climate — a manuscript survives only because somebody copied it before the old one rotted, generation after generation. Everything in this episode reached us because people kept doing that for two thousand years. How does anything written on a palm leaf survive two thousand years?

Episode 26 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
