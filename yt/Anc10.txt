TITLE
Varna, Ashrama and Vedic Society | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 10

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers an altar of layered brick, laid out with real precision. Nothing is worshipped here that can be drawn: there is no image, no figure, only fire and geometry. This episode is about the order that grew up around that fire — an order that organised Indian society for a very long time, and that is still argued about today. Where did this order come from, and how fixed was it to begin with?

Episode 10 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
