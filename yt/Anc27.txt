Trade with Rome and the World | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 27

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a jar with two handles and a pointed base — a shape made for stacking in a ship's hold, and a shape nobody in India was making. It is an amphora, from the Mediterranean, and it was found in India because somebody sailed it here. How did a Mediterranean jar end up buried in India?

Episode 27 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
