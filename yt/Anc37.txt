TITLE
The Rajput Kingdoms | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 37

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a fort, and the first thing to notice is where it sits. It is not on a plain with walls raised around it. It is on a rock outcrop, using the hill as most of the defence and adding walls only where the slope is not steep enough. The gateways are set at angles so attackers cannot charge straight in, and there is a water source inside. Why build where the rock already is?

Episode 37 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
