TITLE
The Kushanas | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 24

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers gold — a thick, heavy coin, well struck. Gold coinage on this scale is new in India, and it is not a sign of conquest so much as of trade. Somebody was earning a great deal of foreign bullion, and this episode is about how. Who was earning this much foreign gold, and how?

Episode 24 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
