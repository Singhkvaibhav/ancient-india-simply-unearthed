TITLE
The Early Vedic Period | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 7

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Here is a strange thing. For the next four episodes, the ground gives us very little: a few post-holes, some plain pottery, no cities, no palaces. And yet we know more about what these people thought than about almost anyone else in the ancient world. Because they left something else entirely — a book, carried in memory for centuries before anyone wrote it down. How do we know so much about what these people thought, and so little about where they lived?

Episode 7 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Early Vedic in four words. Cows, not crops. Chief, not king.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
