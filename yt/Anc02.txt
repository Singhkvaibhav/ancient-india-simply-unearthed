TITLE
Stone Age India | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 2

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush finds a stone, shaped on both faces to a point. Somebody made this. Not a factory, not a workshop — a person, sitting down, striking one rock with another until it did what they wanted. This is the oldest technology in India, and it ran for hundreds of thousands of years. Who were the first Indians, and how do we know anything at all about them?

Episode 2 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
