Ancient India: a Walk Through the Gallery | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 44

SHORT DESCRIPTION
One last sweep of the brush, and what it uncovers is the brush itself, and the trowel, set down. Forty-three episodes ago we started with the question of how anybody knows anything about a period with no newspapers, no archives and no surviving eyewitness. This episode is the answer, walked through one case at a time. How does anybody know any of this?

Episode 44 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
