The Vakatakas and the Deccan | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 32

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers plaster, and on the plaster, paint — a border of flowers and bands, the colours still readable. Almost no ancient Indian painting survives, because paint needs a surface that lasts and a place out of the weather. Inside a cave cut into a cliff, it can. This episode is about the people who paid for those caves. Why does almost all surviving ancient Indian painting sit inside caves?

Episode 32 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
