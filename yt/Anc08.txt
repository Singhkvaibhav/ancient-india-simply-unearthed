TITLE
The Later Vedic Period | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 8

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush finds a bowl: thin-walled, grey, carefully made, with simple designs painted on it. Archaeologists call it Painted Grey Ware, and they find it across the plains between the Ganga and the Yamuna. It belongs to the centuries when the world of the Rigveda turned into something quite different. How does a chief become a king?

Episode 8 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
