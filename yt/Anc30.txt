Gupta Science and Literature | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 30

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers iron — a tall, slender shaft, and the surface is almost clean. Iron left standing in the open for sixteen centuries should be a heap of rust. This one is not, and the reason is chemistry: the composition of the metal forms a protective layer. Whoever made it did not know the chemistry. They knew the craft. Why is sixteen-hundred-year-old iron not a heap of rust?

Episode 30 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
