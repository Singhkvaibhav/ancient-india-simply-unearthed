TITLE
Ancient Indian Science and Mathematics | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 38

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a stone with numbers on it, and one of them is a small circle. That circle is the most useful thing in this episode. It is a symbol for nothing — and without it, no column can be empty, and without empty columns, place value does not work. Somebody had to decide that nothing deserved a sign of its own. Who decided that nothing deserved a sign of its own?

Episode 38 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Zero, surgery, and the spinning earth — three things this tradition worked out.
