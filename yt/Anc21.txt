Mauryan Administration and Decline | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 21

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a drum of sandstone, and the surface is extraordinary — polished to a mirror, holding the lamplight like glass. It is the signature finish of Mauryan stonework. An empire that can do this to stone can do other difficult things too, and this episode is about those. How do you actually run the first empire in the subcontinent?

Episode 21 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
