SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a coin, and it is immediately strange. Indian coins so far have been silver pieces with punched marks and no name. This one carries a head in profile and lettering around the edge. Something has arrived from somewhere else. Why does this coin have a face on it?

Episode 17 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
