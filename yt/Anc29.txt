TITLE
Gupta Administration and Society | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 29

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers two sheets of copper, joined by a ring and engraved on the inside. This is a legal deed — a grant of land, recorded in metal so it would outlast everybody who signed it. Hundreds of these survive, and they are the single most useful source for how early Indian society was changing. What happens when a state gives its own revenue away?

Episode 29 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
