TITLE
Women in Ancient India | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 43

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a tablet with several lines on it, and each line begins with a name. They are the names of donors — women who gave money to have something built, and had their names cut into it. Most of the writing that survives from ancient India was produced by men. Inscriptions like this one are among the places where women appear in their own right, named, doing something. Where do women appear in the record in their own right?

Episode 43 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Prescription is not description. A law book tells you what somebody wanted; an inscription tells you what somebody did.
