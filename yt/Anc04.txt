Harappan Cities | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 4

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A brush sweeps away four and a half thousand years of dust. Underneath lies a brick drain, still in place. In 1921 and 1922, archaeologists began uncovering two forgotten cities, Harappa and Mohenjo-daro. How did people so long ago build cities this well planned?

Episode 4 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.
