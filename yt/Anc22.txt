TITLE
Shungas, Kanvas and Satavahanas | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 22

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a gateway: two posts, a crossbar, and every inch of it carved with small scenes. It is not part of a wall and it holds nothing up. It is there to be walked through and looked at. The empire that could build across the subcontinent is gone — and what replaces it is this: smaller states, and some of the best carving India had yet produced. What replaces an empire that could build across a subcontinent?

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
