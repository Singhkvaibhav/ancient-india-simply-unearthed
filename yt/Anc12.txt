TITLE
The Mahajanapadas | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 12

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush finds silver, stamped with marks, and behind it the cut face of a city wall. Both are new. For roughly a thousand years after the Harappan cities emptied, nobody in India built a city or struck a coin. Around the sixth century BCE, both come back at once — and that is not a coincidence. Why do cities and coins come back at the same moment?

Episode 12 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
