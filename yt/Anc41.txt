TITLE
Land Grants and the Feudalism Debate | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 41

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a boundary stone. Somebody walked the edge of a village, agreed where it ran, and set stones into the ground so the agreement would outlast the memory of it. A boundary stone is a small object with a large implication: somebody now owns what is inside it, and the question of who has been one of the great arguments in Indian history. Who owns what is inside the boundary?

Episode 41 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Grant, intermediary, weaker state — that is the feudalism argument in three words. The counter-argument disputes the third.
