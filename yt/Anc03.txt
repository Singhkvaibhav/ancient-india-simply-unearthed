TITLE
Chalcolithic Cultures | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 3

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush finds the curve of a pot, painted with dark bands on a red body. It is handsome, it is well made, and it was made in a village — not in a city with drains and standard bricks, but in a settlement of a few hundred people who had just started using metal. Why did these villages, so good at farming, never build a city?
