Ashoka and Dhamma | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 19

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a bull, carved on a polished stone capital that once stood on top of a pillar. It is Mauryan, and the polish on it is so fine that for a long time people argued about how it was done. The king who put these up left something rarer than monuments, though — he left his own words. What did a king carve into rock, and why?

Episode 19 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
