SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a floor plan, and it is not a rectangle. It is a star, its points stepping around and around the shrine. A star plan gives a building far more wall surface than a square one would — and if your stone is soft enough to carve like wood, more surface means more room for carving. Why would anyone build a temple on a star-shaped plan?

Episode 36 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
