SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a carved panel: a tree, an empty seat beneath it, a parasol above, and a pair of footprints in front. Someone is clearly the subject of this carving, and he is not in it. For the first few centuries, this is exactly how Buddhist art worked — the Buddha was shown by the space he left. This series keeps that convention throughout. Why is the subject of this carving not in it?

Episode 14 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
