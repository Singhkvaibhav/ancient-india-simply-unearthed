TITLE
Harappan Sites and Decline | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 6

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a small figure in bronze, slender and standing. It was cast by the lost-wax method: model it in wax, cover it in clay, melt the wax out, pour the metal in. Four and a half thousand years ago, somebody in this world knew how to do that. And then, within a few centuries, the cities they lived in emptied. Why did a world this capable empty out its own cities?

Episode 6 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
