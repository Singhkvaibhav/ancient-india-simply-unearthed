TITLE
Chalukyas of Badami and Pallavas | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 33

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a small temple — and it has no joints. It was not built up from blocks; it was carved downward out of a single standing boulder, like a sculpture that happens to be a building. The people who made these were working out how a stone temple should look, in the most difficult way imaginable. How do you carve a building downward out of one boulder?

Episode 33 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Four places: Aihole, Badami, Pattadakal — Chalukya. Mahabalipuram — Pallava.
