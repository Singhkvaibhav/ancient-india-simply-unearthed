TITLE
Vedic Literature | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 9

SHORT DESCRIPTION
A bundle of palm leaves, bound between two wooden boards. Here is the thing to understand before anything else: no original exists. Palm leaf rots. Every manuscript we have is a copy of a copy. What survived was not the object — it was the recitation, repeated exactly, generation after generation, and written down late. Nothing original survives. So how did any of it reach us?

Episode 9 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
