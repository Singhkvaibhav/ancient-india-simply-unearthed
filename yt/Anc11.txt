TITLE
Megalithic Cultures of the South | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 11

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush clears a ring of standing stones with a heavy slab across them. This is not a house and not a temple. It is a grave — and it was built to be seen, by people who wanted the place where they buried their dead to be unmistakable on the landscape, a thousand years before anyone in the south wrote anything down. Who built these, and what happened to them?

Episode 11 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
