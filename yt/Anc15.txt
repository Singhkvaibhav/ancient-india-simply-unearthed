SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a small stupa in stone: a dome, a railing round it, a mast and tiered umbrellas on top. A stupa begins as something very simple — a mound raised over relics — and becomes the central monument of Buddhist architecture. Follow the stupa and you can follow the religion. How does a mound over relics become an architecture?

Episode 15 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
