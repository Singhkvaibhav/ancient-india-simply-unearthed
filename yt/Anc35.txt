TITLE
The Cholas | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 35

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a temple tower, tier stacked on tier. The great one at Thanjavur stands about sixty metres high and its tiers are built of stone fitted without mortar. Building it needed quarries, transport, engineering and money — which means it also needed an administration, and that is the real subject of this episode. What does it take to build sixty metres of stone?

Episode 35 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.
