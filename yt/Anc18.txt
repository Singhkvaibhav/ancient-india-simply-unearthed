TITLE
Chandragupta Maurya and the Arthashastra | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 18

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a manuscript, and it is not a hymn, a story or a prayer. It is a manual: how to raise revenue, how to run a bureaucracy, how to handle spies, famine and war. Somebody in ancient India sat down and wrote the most unsentimental book about power the subcontinent produced. Who sat down and wrote a manual for running a state?

Episode 18 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
King, ministers, territory, fort, treasury, army, ally. Seven limbs of the state.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
