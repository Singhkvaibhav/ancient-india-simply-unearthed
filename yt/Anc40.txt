TITLE
Foreign Travellers | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 40

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a staff and a bundle of written sheets. Everything else in this gallery was made by people who lived here. This was made by somebody passing through, who wrote down what struck him as strange — and what strikes an outsider as strange is often what an insider never thought to record. What does an outsider notice that an insider never records?

Episode 40 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

MEMORY TIP
Five travellers, in order: Megasthenes, Fa-Hien, Hiuen Tsang, I-Tsing, Al-Biruni.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
