TITLE
Jainism | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 16

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a square stone tablet, carved with symbols around a central panel. It is an ayagapata, a votive tablet, and tablets like it were set up at Mathura by Jain donors two thousand years ago. As with the last episode, the revered figure is not drawn here — the tablet is shown for its symbols and its inscription. Who set this up, and what did they believe?

Episode 16 of Ancient India, Simply Unearthed: a calm, five-minute animated explainer for UPSC aspirants, with a memory tip you can carry into the exam hall. English and Hindi audio tracks; subtitles in both.

A NOTE ON SOURCING
Narration follows the approved script word for word, in both languages. Dates are given as the standard textbook dates, with "about" where the sources are uncertain. Where historians genuinely disagree, both readings appear on equal tablets and neither is presented as settled.

No national or state boundaries, no outline of India and no kingdom as shaded territory appear in this episode: sites are pins on a schematic physical board. Nothing revered is depicted as a face or a figure.
