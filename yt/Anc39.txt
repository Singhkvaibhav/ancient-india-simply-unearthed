SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a clay seal, round, with writing around the edge. Seals belong to institutions — a seal exists so that a document can be authenticated as coming from a particular body. An institution with its own seal is an institution that outlives the people in it. What does it mean for an institution to have its own seal?
