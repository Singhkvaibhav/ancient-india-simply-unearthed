The Sangam Kingdoms | Ancient India, Simply Unearthed Ep 25

SHORT DESCRIPTION
The brush uncovers a standing slab, carved with a figure and a few lines of writing. It is a hero stone, set up for someone who died fighting — often, the inscriptions say, defending cattle. Hundreds of these survive across the south, and they are among the earliest inscribed objects in the Tamil country. Who was this stone set up for?
